Full-Time, Four-Year Program

1st & 2nd Semester

1st Semester

Common to Aerospace, Biomedical, Chemical, Civil, Computer, Electrical, Industrial, Mechanical, and Mechatronics Engineering programs.

REQUIRED :

  • Introduction to Engineering
  • * Writing Skills
  • General Chemistry
  • Calculus I
  • Linear Algebra
  • Physics: Mechanics

LIBERAL STUDIES:
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

2nd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • * Introduction to Biomedical Engineering
  • Computer Programming Fundamentals
  • Principles of Engineering Economics
  • Electric Circuit Analysis
  • Calculus II
  • Physics: Waves and Fields

* BME 100 and CEN 199 are graded on a pass/fail basis.

3rd & 4th Semester

3rd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Biology I
  • Biomedical Physics
  • Statics and Mechanics of Materials
  • Digital Systems
  • Differential Equations and Vector Calculus

4th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Physiology
  • Biomechanics
  • Biomaterials
  • Circuits, Devices, and Sensors
  • Communication in the Engineering Professions

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

NOTE: All required courses in 1st and 2nd semester are requisites to all required courses in 3rd semester.

5th & 6th Semester

5th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Anatomy
  • Bioinformatics
  • Introduction to Software
  • Fluid Mechanics
  • Signals and Systems I
  • Microprocessor Systems

6th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Signals and Systems II
  • Electrical Machines and Actuators
  • Control Systems and Bio-Robotics
  • Biomedical Instrumentation
  • Statistics

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table B - Upper Level Liberal Studies .

Note: Students after the 6th semester have options of continuing with the regular program (including, if eligible, enrolling in the Optional Internship Program) or, if eligible, enrolling in the Biomedical Engineering Co-operative Program.

7th & 8th Semester

7th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • * Biomedical Engineering Capstone Design

LIBERAL STUDIES : One course from the following:

  • Science Fiction
  • Technology and the Contemporary Environment
  • Scientific Technology and Modern Society
  • Religion, Science and Philosophy
  • Power, Change and Technology

CORE ELECTIVE : Four courses from the following:

  • Tissue Engineering
  • Radiation Therapy Devices
  • Rehabilitation Engineering
  • Medical Robotics
  • Biomedical Signal Analysis
  • Emerging Topics in Biomedical Engineering

8th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • * Biomedical Engineering Capstone Design
  • Law and Ethics in Engineering Practice

CORE ELECTIVE : Three (3) courses total as follows:

Two (2) courses from the following:

  • Human-Computer Interaction
  • Cardiovascular Biomechanics
  • Biomedical Systems Modelling
  • Biomedical Image Analysis

One (1) course from the following:

  • Design of Bio-MEMS
  • Computations in Genetic Engineering
  • Selected Topics in Engineering

* BME 70A/B is a two-term course and has a GPA weight of 2.0.
