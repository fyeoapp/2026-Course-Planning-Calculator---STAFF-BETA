Full-Time, Four-Year Program

1st & 2nd Semester

1st Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Introduction to Engineering
  • * Writing Skills
  • General Chemistry
  • Calculus I
  • Linear Algebra
  • Physics: Mechanics

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

2nd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Computer Programming Fundamentals
  • Principles of Engineering Economics
  • Engineering Graphical Communication
  • Calculus II
  • Materials Science Fundamentals
  • Physics: Waves and Fields

* This course is graded on a pass/fail basis.

3rd & 4th Semester

3rd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Communication in the Engineering Professions
  • Statics and Mechanics of Materials
  • Introduction to Engineering Design
  • Differential Equations and Vector Calculus
  • Physics: Light and Modern Physics

4th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Electric Circuits
  • Basic Thermodynamics
  • Dynamics
  • Manufacturing Fundamentals
  • Statistics

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

NOTE : All required courses in 1st and 2nd semester are prerequisites to all required courses in 3rd and 4th semester.

5th & 6th Semester

5th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Work Measurement, Analysis and Design
  • Introduction to Data Science and Analytics
  • Operations Research I
  • Experimental Design and Quality Assurance
  • Fluid Mechanics I
  • Numerical Analysis

6th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Introduction to Management
  • Facilities Design
  • Systems Modeling and Simulation
  • Operations Research II
  • Industrial Ergonomics
  • Industrial Psychology

NOTE: All required courses in 1st and 2nd semester are prerequisites to all required courses in 5th and 6th semester.

Students after the 6th semester have options of continuing with the regular program (including, if eligible, enrolling in the Optional Internship Program) or, if eligible, enrolling in the Industrial Engineering Co-operative Program.

7th & 8th Semester

7th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Industrial Systems Design
  • Information Systems
  • Production and Inventory Systems

REQUIRED GROUP 1 : One course from the following:

  • Project Management
  • Flexible Manufacturing Systems
  • Big Data Analytics Project

LIBERAL STUDIES : One course from the following:

  • Science Fiction
  • Technology and the Contemporary Environment
  • Scientific Technology and Modern Society
  • Religion, Science and Philosophy
  • Power, Change and Technology

8th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Law and Ethics in Engineering Practice
  • Industrial Systems Design

REQUIRED GROUP 1 : Two courses from the following:

  • Selected Topics in Engineering
  • Big Data Analytics Tools
  • Service Operations Management
  • Reliability and Decision Analysis
  • Financial Engineering

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table B - Upper Level Liberal Studies .

NOTE : All required courses in 1st through 4th semester are prerequisites to all required courses in 7th and 8th semester.
