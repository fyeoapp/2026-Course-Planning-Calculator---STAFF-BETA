Computer Engineering - Common First Two Years

1st & 2nd Semester

1st Semester

Common to Aerospace, Biomedical, Chemical, Civil, Computer, Electrical, Industrial, Mechanical, and Mechatronics Engineering programs.

REQUIRED :

  • Introduction to Engineering
  • * Writing Skills
  • General Chemistry
  • Calculus I
  • Linear Algebra
  • Physics: Mechanics

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

2nd Semester

Common to Computer and Electrical Engineering programs.

REQUIRED :

  • Computer Programming Fundamentals
  • Principles of Engineering Economics
  • Electric Circuit Analysis
  • Calculus II
  • Physics: Waves and Fields

* is graded in a pass/fail basis.

3rd & 4th Semester

Common to all students in Computer Engineering.

3rd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Software Systems
  • Digital Systems
  • Electric Networks
  • Differential Equations and Vector Calculus
  • Solid State Physics

4th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Communication in the Engineering Professions
  • Engineering Algorithms and Data Structures
  • Object Oriented Eng Analysis and Design
  • Electronic Circuits I
  • Discrete Mathematics for Engineers

  • Communication Systems

One course from Table B - Upper Level Liberal Studies .
