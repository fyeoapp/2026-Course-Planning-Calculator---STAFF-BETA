Full-Time, Four-Year Program

1st & 2nd Semester

1st Semester

Common to Aerospace, Biomedical, Chemical, Civil, Computer, Electrical, Industrial, Mechanical, and Mechatronics Engineering programs.

REQUIRED :

  • Introduction to Engineering
  • * Writing Skills
  • General Chemistry
  • Calculus I
  • Linear Algebra
  • Physics: Mechanics

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

* This course is graded on a pass/fail basis.

2nd Semester

Common to Industrial, Mechanical, and Mechatronics Engineering programs.

REQUIRED :

  • Computer Programming Fundamentals
  • Principles of Engineering Economics
  • Engineering Graphical Communication
  • Calculus II
  • Materials Science Fundamentals
  • Physics: Waves and Fields

3rd & 4th Semester

3rd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Communication in the Engineering Professions
  • Statics and Mechanics of Materials
  • Introduction to Engineering Design
  • Programming for Mechatronics Engineering
  • Differential Equations and Vector Calculus

4th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Dynamics
  • Manufacturing Fundamentals
  • Thermodynamics and Fluids
  • Analysis of Electric Circuits
  • Statistics

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

5th & 6th Semester

5th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Electric Machines and Actuators
  • Project Management
  • Signals and Systems for Mechatronics Engineering
  • Microprocessor Systems
  • Numerical Analysis

6th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Control Systems
  • Machine Mechanics and Design
  • Introduction to Robotics
  • Computer Structures and Real-Time Systems
  • Electromechanical System Modelling and Design
  • Measurements, Sensors and Instruments

Students after the 6th semester have options of continuing with the regular program (including, if eligible, enrolling in the Optional Internship Program) or, if eligible, enrolling in the Mechatronics Engineering Co-operative Program.

7th & 8th Semester

7th Semester

REQUIRED:

  • Mechatronics Capstone Design
  • Real-Time Digital Control Systems Design

REQUIRED GROUP 1 : Two courses from the following:

  • Manufacturing System Controls
  • Fundamentals of Microelectromechanical Systems
  • Sensor Fusion
  • Digital Signal Processing
  • System Models and Identification

LIBERAL STUDIES : One course from the following:

  • Science Fiction
  • Technology and the Contemporary Environment
  • Scientific Technology and Modern Society
  • Religion, Science and Philosophy
  • Power, Change and Technology

8th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Law and Ethics in Engineering Practice
  • Mechatronics Capstone Design
  • Hardware in the Loop and RCP

REQUIRED GROUP 1: Two courses from the following:

  • Selected Topics in Engineering
  • Design of Modern Control Systems
  • Autonomous Robots and Vehicles
  • Control of Robotic Manipulators
  • Introduction to Digital Image Processing
  • Intelligent Systems

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table B - Upper Level Liberal Studies .
