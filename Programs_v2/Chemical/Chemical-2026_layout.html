Full-Time, Five-Year Co-Op Program

Co-operative students see Sequence of Academic and Work Terms for Co-op Program.

1st & 2nd Semester

1st Semester

Common to Aerospace, Biomedical, Chemical, Civil, Computer, Electrical, Industrial, Mechanical, and Mechatronics Engineering programs.

REQUIRED :

  • Introduction to Engineering
  • * Writing Skills
  • General Chemistry
  • Calculus I
  • Linear Algebra
  • Physics: Mechanics

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

2nd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Chemical Engineering Fundamentals
  • General Chemistry Laboratory
  • Digital Computation and Programming
  • Principles of Engineering Economics
  • Calculus II
  • Physics: Waves and Fields

* CEN 199 is graded on a pass/fail basis.

3rd & 4th Semester

3rd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Thermodynamics I
  • Fluid Mechanics
  • Organic Chemistry
  • Electric Circuits
  • Differential Equations and Vector Calculus

4th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Thermodynamics II
  • Process Measurements
  • Heat Transfer
  • Instrumental Methods of Analysis
  • Communication in the Engineering Professions

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

5th & 6th Semester

5th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Chemical Engineering Materials
  • Mass Transfer
  • Separation Processes
  • Engineering Statistics and Probabilities
  • Chemical Engineering Computations

LIBERAL STUDIES : One course from the following:

  • Science Fiction
  • Technology and the Contemporary Environment
  • Scientific Technology and Modern Society
  • Religion, Science and Philosophy
  • Power, Change and Technology

6th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Introductory Bioengineering
  • Chemical Reaction Engineering
  • Unit Operations Laboratory I
  • Process Modeling and Simulation
  • Chemical Process Safety Loss Prevention

CORE ELECTIVE : One course from Table I .

Note:  All 1st, 2nd and 3rd year required courses must be successfully completed in order to be eligible to begin the co-op placements.
