Full-Time, Four-Year Program

1st & 2nd Semester

Common to Aerospace, Chemical and Civil Engineering Programs.

1st Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Introduction to Engineering
  • * Writing Skills
  • General Chemistry
  • Calculus I
  • Linear Algebra
  • Physics: Mechanics

LIBERAL STUDIES:
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

2nd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Engineering Design and Graphical Communication
  • Digital Computation and Programming
  • Principles of Engineering Economics
  • Calculus II
  • Materials Science Fundamentals
  • Physics: Waves and Fields

* This course is graded in a pass/fail basis.

3rd & 4th Semester

3rd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Basic Thermodynamics
  • Fluid Mechanics
  • Dynamics
  • Statics and Intro to Strength of Materials
  • Communication in the Engineering Professions
  • Differential Equations and Vector Calculus

4th Semester

REQUIRED:

  • Mechanisms and Vibrations
  • Intro to Aerospace Engineering Design
  • Flight Mechanics
  • Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer
  • Electric Circuits
  • Statistics

NOTE: All required courses in 1st and 2nd semester are prerequisites to all required courses in 3rd & 4th Semester.

5th & 6th Semester

Revised curriculum begins 2022-2023 for students admitted Fall 2020 and after.

5th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Aerodynamics
  • Materials and Manufacturing
  • Stress Analysis
  • Electric Machines and Actuators
  • Numerical Analysis

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

6th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Control Systems
  • Component Design and Material Selection
  • Aircraft Performance
  • Aerospace Structural Design
  • Gas Dynamics

CORE ELECTIVE : One course from the following. Students must complete the requirements for one of the following Streams:

Aircraft Stream

  • Applied Finite Elements

Avionics Stream

  • Avionics and Sensors

Spacecraft Stream

  • Orbital Dynamics

All required courses in 1st and 2nd semester are prerequisites to all required courses in 5th & 6th Semester.

NOTE: Students after the 6th semester have options of continuing with the regular program (including, if eligible, enrolling in the Optional Internship Program) or, if eligible, enrolling in the Aerospace Engineering Co-operative Program.
