Full-Time, Four-Year Program and Five-Year Co-op Program

Co-operative students see Sequence of Academic and Work Terms for Co-op Program.

1st & 2nd Semester

1st Semester

Common to Aerospace, Biomedical, Chemical, Civil, Computer, Electrical, Industrial, Mechanical, and Mechatronics Engineering programs.

REQUIRED :

  • Introduction to Engineering
  • * Writing Skills
  • General Chemistry
  • Calculus I
  • Linear Algebra
  • Physics: Mechanics

LIBERAL STUDIES:
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

2nd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Engineering Design and Graphical Communication
  • Digital Computation and Programming
  • Principles of Engineering Economics
  • Calculus II
  • Materials Science Fundamentals
  • Physics: Waves and Fields

* This course is graded on a pass/fail basis.

3rd & 4th Semester

3rd Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Basic Thermodynamics
  • Fluid Mechanics
  • Dynamics
  • Statics and Intro to Strength of Materials
  • Communication in the Engineering Professions
  • Differential Equations and Vector Calculus

4th Semester

REQUIRED:

  • Mechanisms and Vibrations
  • Intro to Aerospace Engineering Design
  • Flight Mechanics
  • Thermodynamics and Heat Transfer
  • Electric Circuits
  • Statistics

NOTE: All required courses in 1st and 2nd semester are prerequisites to all required courses in 3rd & 4th Semester.

5th & 6th Semester

5th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Aerodynamics
  • Materials and Manufacturing
  • Stress Analysis
  • Electric Machines and Actuators
  • Numerical Analysis

LIBERAL STUDIES :
One course from Table A - Lower Level Liberal Studies .

6th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Control Systems
  • Component Design and Material Selection
  • Aircraft Performance
  • Aerospace Structural Design
  • Gas Dynamics

CORE ELECTIVE : One course from the following. Students must complete the requirements for one of the following Streams:

Aircraft Stream

  • Applied Finite Elements

Avionics Stream

  • Aerospace Sensors

Spacecraft Stream

  • Orbital Dynamics

All required courses in 1st and 2nd semester are prerequisites to all required courses in 5th & 6th Semester.

NOTE: Students after the 6th semester have options of continuing with the regular program (including, if eligible, enrolling in the Optional Internship Program) or, if eligible, enrolling in the Aerospace Engineering Co-operative Program.

7th & 8th Semester

7th Semester

LIBERAL STUDIES: One course from the following:

  • Science Fiction
  • Technology and the Contemporary Environment
  • Scientific Technology and Modern Society
  • Religion, Science and Philosophy
  • Power, Change and Technology

Aircraft Stream Required :

  • Avionics and Systems
  • Aircraft Stability and Control
  • Aeroelasticity

Aircraft Stream Core Elective :

One course from the following:

  • Systems Engineering
  • Composite Materials
  • Introduction to Machine Learning

Avionics Stream Required :

  • Avionics and Systems
  • Aircraft Stability and Control
  • Digital Systems

Avionics Stream Core Elective :

One course from the following:

  • Systems Engineering
  • Composite Materials
  • Introduction to Machine Learning

Spacecraft Stream Required :

  • Introduction to Space Systems Design
  • Systems Engineering
  • Spacecraft Attitude Dynamics and Control

Spacecraft Stream Core Elective :

One course from the following:

  • Avionics and Systems
  • Composite Materials
  • Introduction to Machine Learning

8th Semester

REQUIRED :

  • Propulsion
  • Law and Ethics in Engineering Practice

LIBERAL STUDIES:
One course from Table B - Upper Level Liberal Studies .

CORE ELECTIVE† : One course from the following:

  • Introduction to Space Robotics
  • Manufacturing Management
  • Aerospace Engineering Thesis
  • Selected Topics in Engineering

Aircraft Stream Required :

  • Aircraft Design Project

Avionics Stream Required :

  • Avionics Design Project

Spacecraft Stream Required :

  • Space Systems Design Project

NOTE: All required courses in 1st through 4th semester are prerequisites to all required courses in 7th & 8th Semester.

NOTE: Students after the 6th semester have options of continuing with the regular program (including, if eligible, enrolling in the Optional Internship Program) or, if eligible, enrolling in the Aerospace Engineering Co-operative Program.

† Not every course will be offered every year.
